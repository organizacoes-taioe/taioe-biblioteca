::rubrica A park near the palace.

::rubrica Enter three Murderers.

::fala First Murderer
But who did bid thee join with us?

::fala Third Murderer
                                   Macbeth.

::fala Second Murderer
He needs not our mistrust; since he delivers
Our offices, and what we have to do,
To the direction just.

::fala First Murderer
                       Then stand with us.
The west yet glimmers with some streaks of day:
Now spurs the lated traveller apace
To gain the timely inn, and near approaches
The subject of our watch.

::fala Third Murderer
                          Hark! I hear horses.

::fala Banquo
[Within] Give us a light there, ho!

::fala Second Murderer
                                    Then 'tis he: the rest
That are within the note of expectation
Already are i' the court.

::fala First Murderer
                          His horses go about.

::fala Third Murderer
Almost a mile: but he does usually—
So all men do—from hence to the palace gate
Make it their walk.

::fala Second Murderer
                    A light, a light!

::rubrica Enter Banquo, and Fleance with a torch.

::fala Third Murderer
                                      'Tis he.

::fala First Murderer
Stand to 't.

::fala Banquo
It will be rain to-night.

::fala First Murderer
                          Let it come down.

::rubrica They set upon Banquo.

::fala Banquo
O, treachery! Fly, good Fleance, fly, fly, fly!
Thou mayst revenge. O slave! [Dies. Fleance escapes.]

::fala Third Murderer
Who did strike out the light?

::fala First Murderer
                              Was't not the way?

::fala Third Murderer
There's but one down; the son is fled.

::fala Second Murderer
                                       We have lost
Best half of our affair.

::fala First Murderer
Well, let's away and say how much is done. [Exeunt.]
