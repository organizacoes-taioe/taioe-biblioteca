::rubrica Another part of the field.

::rubrica Alarums. Enter Macbeth.

::fala Macbeth
They have tied me to a stake; I cannot fly,
But bear-like I must fight the course. What's he
That was not born of woman? Such a one
Am I to fear, or none.

::rubrica Enter young Siward.

::fala Young Siward
What is thy name?

::fala Macbeth
                  Thou'lt be afraid to hear it.

::fala Young Siward
No; though thou call'st thyself a hotter name
Than any is in hell.

::fala Macbeth
                     My name 's Macbeth.

::fala Young Siward
The devil himself could not pronounce a title
More hateful to mine ear.

::fala Macbeth
                          No, nor more fearful.

::fala Young Siward
Thou liest, abhorred tyrant; with my sword
I'll prove the lie thou speak'st.

::rubrica They fight, and young Siward is slain.

::fala Macbeth
                                  Thou wast born of woman.
But swords I smile at, weapons laugh to scorn,
Brandish'd by man that's of a woman born. [Exit.]

::rubrica Alarums. Enter Macduff.

::fala Macduff
That way the noise is. Tyrant, show thy face!
If thou be'st slain and with no stroke of mine,
My wife and children's ghosts will haunt me still.
I cannot strike at wretched kerns, whose arms
Are hired to bear their staves: either thou, Macbeth,
Or else my sword, with an unbatter'd edge,
I sheathe again undeeded. There thou shouldst be;
By this great clatter, one of greatest note
Seems bruited: let me find him, fortune!
And more I beg not. [Exit. Alarums.]

::rubrica Enter Malcolm and old Siward.

::fala Siward
This way, my lord; the castle's gently render'd:
The tyrant's people on both sides do fight;
The noble thanes do bravely in the war;
The day almost itself professes yours,
And little is to do.

::fala Malcolm
                     We have met with foes
That strike beside us.

::fala Siward
                       Enter, sir, the castle.

::rubrica Exeunt. Alarum.
