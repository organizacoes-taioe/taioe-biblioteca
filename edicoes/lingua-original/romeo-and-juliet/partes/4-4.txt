::rubrica Hall in Capulet's house.

::rubrica Enter Lady Capulet and Nurse.

::fala Lady Capulet
Hold, take these keys, and fetch more spices, nurse.

::fala Nurse
They call for dates and quinces in the pastry.

::rubrica Enter Capulet.

::fala Capulet
Come, stir, stir, stir! the second cock hath crow'd,
The curfew-bell hath rung, 'tis three o'clock:
Look to the baked meats, good Angelica:
Spare not for cost.

::fala Nurse
                    Go, you cot-quean, go,
Get you to bed; faith, you'll be sick to-morrow
For this night's watching.

::fala Capulet
No, not a whit: what! I have watch'd ere now
All night for lesser cause, and ne'er been sick.

::fala Lady Capulet
Ay, you have been a mouse-hunt in your time;
But I will watch you from such watching now.

::rubrica Exeunt Lady Capulet and Nurse.

::fala Capulet
A jealous-hood, a jealous-hood!

::rubrica Enter three or four Servingmen, with spits, and logs, and baskets.

                                Now, fellow,
What's there?

::fala First Servant
Things for the cook, sir, but I know not what.

::fala Capulet
Make haste, make haste. [Exit First Servant.] Sirrah, fetch drier logs:
Call Peter, he will show thee where they are.

::fala Second Servant
I have a head, sir, that will find out logs,
And never trouble Peter for the matter.

::fala Capulet
Mass, and well said; a merry whoreson, ha!
Thou shalt be logger-head. [Exit Second Servant.] Good faith, 'tis day:
The county will be here with music straight,
For so he said he would. [Music within] I hear him near.
Nurse! Wife! What, ho! What, nurse, I say!

::rubrica Re-enter Nurse.

Go waken Juliet, go and trim her up;
I'll go and chat with Paris: hie, make haste,
Make haste: the bridegroom he is come already:
Make haste, I say. [Exeunt.]
