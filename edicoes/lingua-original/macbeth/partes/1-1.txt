::rubrica A desert place.

::rubrica Thunder and lightning. Enter three Witches.

::fala First Witch
When shall we three meet again
In thunder, lightning, or in rain?

::fala Second Witch
When the hurlyburly's done,
When the battle's lost and won.

::fala Third Witch
That will be ere the set of sun.

::fala First Witch
Where the place?

::fala Second Witch
                 Upon the heath.

::fala Third Witch
There to meet with Macbeth.

::fala First Witch
I come, Graymalkin.

::fala All
Paddock calls:—anon!
Fair is foul, and foul is fair.
Hover through the fog and filthy air. [Exeunt.]
